# The Fractured Archive

### Boss 1: Archive Guardian
#### Gear:
* Serp or anti-venom for mushrooms
* T-bow/bowfa for ranging
* Crush weapon swap for melee phase
#### Mechanics:
* Pop shrooms by stepping within 1 tile for 1 tick and then moving away 2 tiles (maybe 1 tile?)
* Boss targets a single player. That player should pray range. Other players should stand to the left and right of where the boss is facing to not take damage. Standing behind the boss takes melee damage.
* Once boss' shield is down, he slams down. Stand 2 tiles away and spam click to attack him once he falls down. If you are 1 tile away you take damage.
* Rotate clockwise as a team while damaging the boss to avoid tentacles
* Once boss health is low enough, it enrages and shoots death beams.
* Pray mage to reduce death beam damage.
* Destroying the torches damages the boss. The death beams one shot the torches.

#### Gear:
* Bludgeon for melee/crush
* ACB/blow pipe for range

#### Mechanics:
* One player is targeted by Bork and the other by Snorks.
* Kill Bork first, and then Snorks will attack similar to Zebak's enrage.
* Stun Bork by hitting him with the big cannon from the Snorks.
* Snorks attack with two patterns:
    1. Auto-Auto-Fire-Auto-Cannon
    1. Auto-Auto-Auto-Fire-Auto-Auto-Cannon
* Firey gloop leaves gloop on ground that causes burn damage. Player targeted by Snorks should aim this away from the center.

### Boss 3:
{{#tabs }}
{{#tab name="Dreadnoth the Jellied" }}
#### Gear:
* Demon - try using T-Bow and Scorching Bow

#### Mechanics:
* Slams do melee damage. The objects that shoot off after a slam do range
* Jellies do melee damage. If not killed they will heal the boss when he jumps on theme.
* Jellies can be blood barraged for free heals
* On death jellies drop blood splats that cause bleed damage. Try and stack them to keep the ground clear
